Nothing below 11px — the old app's 6–10px floor is retired. Serif carries identity; sans does the work; mono marks machine-shaped values.

Structure

rules over boxes, tone over shadow
2px ink rule — anchors mastheads, stat strips, footers
1px strong hairline — panel edges, column splits
1px hairline — table rows, list separators
Spacing rhythm: 4 · 8 · 12 · 20 · 32 · 56 (56 = page margin)
Radii: 3px chips & swatches · 6px panels — near-square, print-like
Figures end with a caption — a short italic line naming what the reader sees. No numbering, anywhere.
